Orkz.
Da orkz are da orkyest boyz in all of da warp, bad, garg an’ orky.
Any of da boyz grows garg over time after dredging da skum.
Every time you finish a great battle you gain +1 str and +1 con and gain an additional feet of height
Every boy starts with an average intelligence of 6 and it grows 1 point for every million orks involved in da collective waaaagh.


Oddboyz:
The oddboyz are da mek an’ manik of da boyz in warhammer 40k, da oddboyz are really weird wit either psychic powers, engineering prowess and medical knowledge exclusive to orks. Da stash to their inherent knowledge resides within their DNA.

Level 1: Da WAAAAAAAAAAAAGH: 
Beginning at level 1, every oddboy has a chance to either be a mekboy, a painboy or an eadbanga. Each one has completely different skills. Normal boyz can specialize in da oder stuff like being herdas, madboyz, wildboyz, pigdoks, runtherds, speed freeks, sumboyz, yellerz an’ brewboyz.

Skills.
Herdas
Da herdas specialize in taking care of squigs an’ have advantage on animal handling rolls aswell as handling da trappas to catch squigs.
Madboyz
Da Madboyz are a lil off in da head, they dont do nothin da orky way, too manik, they have an extra 1d6 to initiative and if they are the first ones to act in any stealth or formation they will break it.
Wildboyz
Da wildboyz stay exploring instead of going to da dakka, they know their way around and have advantage in survival rolls.
Pigdoks
Da pigdoks hang with da wildboyz and make their squigs stronger with their medicine, any squiga affected by a pigdok grows bigger and doubles hit points.
Runtherds
Da Runtherds handle da grots and they always have gretchin an’ snotling aswell as any other grots under their command.
Speed freeks
Da speed freeks love karts and if a speed freek is driving they have advantage in dexterity saving throws for da kart or vroom.
Sumboyz
Da sumboyz handle teef and know how to sum and stuff, they can count and have +2 intelligence to normal boyz.
Yellerz
Da yellerz track about da Gogz Gork an’ Mork, they have advantage on religion saving throws and they can inspire da boyz’s morale.
Brewboyz
Da brewboyz cook really orky an’ make da boyz happy.

Level 3: Fungi go!
At 3rd level da orkz begin to release spores on da planetas they visit, allowing for a massive wave of orks to continuously attack the planet.

Level 5: Stronger waagh!
Any 5th level ork will gain a temporary +2 to strength and 15 additional hit points if they are in an active waagh.
Multiattack (1)

Level 8: Naa Skab Orks.
Level 8 orks will gain an additional feet in size and +1 str +1 con everytime they survive grievous injuries.
Also orkz can choose to charge without concern. Enemies get advantage in attacks against the ork but the ork gainz temporal HP= Con mod* Orkz in a 5ft radius and gains advantage on enemies in that turn. The HP only lasts until the end of the round and they can only take this action a number of times equal to CON modifier.
Level 14: Nob time.
At level 14, orks become nobs and can command da other boyz with more ease. +1d10 to gather weaker orks without having to fight. Also nobs can extort weaker orkz to gain their teef

Level 16: Boss hour.
Starting at level 16, you become the very bosses you used to serve, you can start your own waaagh and gain a +1d20 to gather orks without having to fight. Also orkz from other planets start joining voluntarily to your crew if you have a waaagh of over 5 million orkz.
Mekboys:
Level 1: Green tweakz
Starting from a mekboy’s maturity, their mek starts in a small proportion. As an action they can add certain but only one aspectz to weaponz and Kartz (Vehicles), as well as dur, as long as he has his mek tools in hand,  granting each item a special buff. This power grants you the title of Spanner. The number of modifications for items is equal to the proficiency bonus plus half the levels in Mekboy (Rounded up). A mek can recover the uses of this ability after a long rest. The following modifications for the items are the following.

Red Paint
Red paint to any vehicles or units gives them a 1.5x boost in movement speed
Blue Paint
Blue Paint gives a +3 bonus to any saving throw, if the saving throw is a success the painting falls off.
Purple Paint
Purple paint on units allows them to throw an extra 1d6 for stealth checks. If the check is a success the paint falls off
Yellow Paint
Yellow paint adds a 1.5x to damage
Black and white grid
The grids add a +2 to initiative when orks initiate combat
Car spikes
Adds a 1d8 to damage when running someone over with a car with spikes and prevents the car from losing momentum
Black Paint
Adding black paint to any unit will increase their toughness and give them 1d12 temporal hit points


Level 3: Da big gunz
Beginning at third level, your lowly Spanner becomes a worthy Mek and finally begins to make dakka for da boyz. The new Mek can now make shootas, choppas and stompaz, as well as weapons of a basic caliber, aswell as team up with 3 other meks to make basic karts.

Level 6: Flow with da Doks and keep gettin’ loot.
Starting at level 6, da mek can now build prosthetics for da painboyz and has advantage when scavenging for loot. Also you can attune to items up to a number equal to INT mod. Attuned weapons have a +1 to attack rolls and shields get a +1 AC
Level 9: More dakka! An’ gargants.
Meks can build weapons of medium caliber and can be summoned to work in da gargants for nobs. If they build basic caliber weapons they have either 1.2x range or and additional 2d6 damage.

Level 14: Da mek becomes a big-mek
Big-mekz can build gargants with less companions to aid them, they can also build high caliber dakka, start mek factories and can put more than one tweak to stuff.

Level 16: Mek-boss.
As a mek-boss you can use the powers of any waaagh you start and use them to construct any machine with near limitless power. (Check with the DM) Limits decrease as you gain more levels.

Notes: Any mek can make any build but the time, money and effort needed are insanely larger if you are a low level mek.
